## Evidence and engineering terms

| Term | Meaning | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Fact | A recorded event or state with source and context; not automatically a conclusion. | Authorization, correctness or production readiness. |
| Exact knowledge version | An immutable knowledge identity plus version and digest used by an evaluation. | That the knowledge is universally true or currently applicable. |
| Governance evaluation | A deterministic calculation over facts, knowledge and explicit rules or boundaries. | Authority to execute the recommended action. |
| Evidence | Material that supports inspection and replay. | Authorization or final truth by itself. |
| Authorization | Permission granted by an accountable human, organization or external system. | That an action was executed or succeeded. |
| Observation | A bounded, explainable finding. | An automatic punishment, production command or final ruling. |
| Replay | Reconstructing an evaluation with pinned inputs, versions and rules. | General compatibility outside the pinned scope. |
| UNKNOWN | An explicit result when evidence or knowledge is insufficient. | A hidden success or permission to guess. |
| Roadmap | Planned direction. | Implementation, verification or release. |

## Concept and architecture terms

These terms explain design intent. Their names do not upgrade a concept into an implemented capability.

| Term | Plain meaning | Current classification |
|---|---|---|
| Compassion Protocol | A design principle that asks governance to preserve continuity, choice, difference and repair where possible. | Conceptual and protocol research; use repository contracts and tests for engineering claims. |
| Cell Protocol | A proposed local declaration of identity, capability, boundary and responsibility for a system or organization. | Partly represented in protocol drafts and examples; not a claim of a production network. |
| Guardian Network | A proposed model for bounded, plural review rather than one unlimited judge. | Architecture direction; not a deployed global reviewer network. |
| Dream Butterfly / boundary self-knowledge | A metaphor for explicitly representing uncertainty and limits of interpretation or authority. | Conceptual source; engineered behavior must be shown through fields, rules and tests such as `UNKNOWN`. |
| Full-spectrum / holographic recursion | A research view that similar governance invariants can recur at different subject and organizational scales. | Non-normative research interpretation; not runtime evidence. |

Use the [Evidence and Project Status](https://github.com/full-spectrum-lab/full-spectrum-commons/blob/main/docs/evidence-and-status.md) vocabulary when making public capability claims.
